# IFU income items

This reference groups common *Imprimé fiscal unique* (IFU) items by the related French income-tax return box. Titles are English descriptions for the application UI.

## Interest and dividends

| Tax return box | English title |
| --- | --- |
| 2TR | Interest and other fixed-income investment income |
| 2TT | Interest from participatory loans and minibonds |
| 2DC | Share and unit income eligible for the 40% allowance when the progressive tax scale is selected |

## Withholding tax and social contributions

| Tax return box | English title |
| --- | --- |
| 2CG | Income already subject to social contributions, without deductible CSG |
| 2BH | Income already subject to social contributions, with deductible CSG when the progressive tax scale is selected |
| 2CK | Non-final flat-rate withholding tax already paid |
| 2DF | Other income already subject to social contributions, with deductible CSG |

## Life insurance and capitalization contracts

| Tax return box | English title |
| --- | --- |
| 2DH | Life-insurance and capitalization-contract proceeds subject to 7.5% final withholding tax |
| 2YY | Other proceeds from life-insurance and capitalization contracts of less than eight years, for contributions made before 27 September 2017 |
| 2ZZ | Proceeds from life-insurance and capitalization contracts of less than eight years, for contributions made from 27 September 2017 |
